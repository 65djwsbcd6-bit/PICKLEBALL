## 1. Project Overview & Scope
This project translates previous academic research into three periodized microcycles for Pickleball:
- **Milestone 1 — Biomechanics Breakdown:** Forehand Drive (3rd class lever, kinetic chain GRF), Overhead Smash (SSC, >1000°/s shoulder internal rotation), and Dink Shot & Split Step (low COG, 60°–90° knee flexion, <0.4s reaction window).
- **Milestone 2 — Exercise Selection:** 6 original exercises, baseline vs. current measurements, and 8-week aerobic & court agility goal (20 to 45 minutes at 65–75% HRmax).
- **Final Microcycle Architecture:**
  - **Phase 01 — Preparation:** "BUILD THE FOUNDATION" (Foundational volume, 12–15 reps, 60–70% HRmax).
  - **Phase 02 — Pre-Competition:** "TURN STRENGTH INTO POWER" (Rate of force development, plyometrics, 70–80% HRmax, reactive split-step).
  - **Phase 03 — Competition:** "PERFORM WHEN IT MATTERS" (Tapered volume, neural priming, match day readiness).
